## Business Description (max ~750 characters)
Remote Control Cafe (RCC) is Auckland's most unique indoor activity. Take the
controls of real remote control construction machines — diggers, dump trucks,
cranes and loaders — and put them to work in our purpose-built indoor arena,
then relax in the cafe with coffee, cold drinks and toasties. It's hands-on,
screen-free fun for kids, families and groups, and it's fully indoors so it
works rain or shine. Perfect for kids activities, rainy days, birthday parties,
school holidays, family outings and corporate team building. Based on Mount
Eden Road, we welcome guests from across Auckland. Weekday sessions book online;
weekends, holidays and groups by enquiry.
